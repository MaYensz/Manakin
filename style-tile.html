Style

This page documents every aspect of the design system used across this website: palette, typographic scale, and spacing rules.

Palette

Primary, Mauve shadow #673245. Header background and heading color. Lavender Blush #F7F3F5 on this background checks at 9.01 to 1

Neutral Graphite text #303030 on neutral background #F7F3F5. Body text and page background. Checks at 11.99 to 1

CTA background, Sunflower Gold, #a8541f. Graphite text on this background checks at 7.13 to 1.

Type Scale

Heading 1, 2.25rem (36px)

Heading 2, 1.5rem (24px)

Body text, 1rem (16px)

Spacing

  • *: 0 margin and padding on all elements to remove browser default spacing.
  • class="clinic-title": margin-bottom 0 to remove default h1 spacing below the clinic title.
  • main: max-width 1100px, padding 40px top and bottom, 20px left and right. margin 0 auto to center the main content on the page.
  • body: min-height 100vh to ensure the footer is always at the bottom of the page.
  • header: padding 20px in all directions, to separate the header from the page edges.
  • header nav a: margin-right 16px to separate links from each other.
  • The book an appointment button: padding 8px top and bottom, 16px left and right, to make the button larger and easier to click.
  • h1: margin-bottom 36px to separate the heading from the content below.
  • h2: margin-bottom 8px to separate the subheading from the content below.
  • p: margin-bottom 32px to separate paragraphs from each other.
  • section class="hero":
    hero: padding 60px top and bottom, 40px left and right, to separate the hero section from the page edges.
    hero-content: max-width 600px to limit the width of the hero content.
    hero-welcome: margin-bottom 10px to separate the welcome message from the title.
    hero-title: margin 0 to remove default spacing around the title.
    hero-secondary: margin-bottom 10px to separate the secondary message
  • footer: padding 20px in all directions, margin-top auto to separate the footer from the main content, and to push the footer to the bottom of the page.
