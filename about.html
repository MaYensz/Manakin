About Us

With over 30 years of experience, Manakin Sabot Veterinary Clinic is a small, privately owned veterinary clinic located in Manakin Sabot, Virginia. Located just minutes west of Short Pump, our clinic serves as a vital resource for pet owners across Henrico, Goochland, Powhatan and Midlothian.

Our clinic is managed by a team of veterinary doctors, who provide compassionate, high-quality care for dogs and cats. We specialize in routine wellness exams, vaccination, preventative health and routine dental care, ensuring your pet always receives quality medical care.

Our clinic operates by appointment only. Give us a call to book your pet's exam in advance.
